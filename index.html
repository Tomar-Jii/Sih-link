⚠️ Important Note for Evaluators

Our backend services are currently hosted on Render's Free Tier. Due to platform policies, free instances automatically "spin down" (go to sleep) after 15 minutes of inactivity.

As a result, the first request may take 50-60 seconds to wake the server up. To ensure a seamless evaluation experience, we have provided a redundant Backup Server. If the main server is loading, you can instantly switch to the backup link.
